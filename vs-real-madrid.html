Frequently Asked Questions: Messi vs Real Madrid

How many goals did Messi score against Real Madrid? ▾

Lionel Messi scored 26 goals in 45 matches against Real Madrid: 18 in La Liga, 6 in the Supercopa, and 2 in the Champions League. He is the all-time top scorer in El Clásico history.

How many assists did Messi get against Real Madrid? ▾

Messi registered 14 assists against Real Madrid, taking his total goal contributions to 40 in 45 matches (0.89 goal contributions per game).

Who has more El Clásico goals: Messi or Cristiano Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi scored 26 goals in El Clásico matches compared to Cristiano Ronaldo's 18 goals for Real Madrid.

Where did Messi celebrate with his shirt held up? ▾

At the Santiago Bernabéu on 23 April 2017, after scoring his 500th Barcelona goal in the 92nd minute to win El Clásico 3–2.
