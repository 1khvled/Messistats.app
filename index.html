Lionel Messi has won 8 Ballons d'Or (2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, 2023) — an all-time record in football history.

Messi has scored 129 goals in 163 Champions League games (120 for Barcelona, 9 for PSG) and won 4 UCL titles (2006, 2009, 2011, 2015). View full data on our Champions League hub.

What are Messi's FIFA World Cup statistics?▾
