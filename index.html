Quick answers

How many goals has Messi scored? 927 senior goals in 1,172 appearances: 802 in 965 club games plus 125 in 207 Argentina caps, cross-checked Sep 2026.

Which club has he scored most for? Barcelona, by distance: 672 goals in 778 games (2004-2021).

What was his best season? 2011/12 at Barcelona: 73 goals in 60 games. Best calendar year: 2012 with 91 goals.

Where do these numbers come from? Season splits from Wikipedia/BDFutbol tables, Argentina years confirmed against ESPN milestones, World Cup goals recounted from openfootball JSON. Full method in the data file.
