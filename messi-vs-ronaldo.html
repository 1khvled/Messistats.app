Who has better all-time career statistics: Messi or Ronaldo?

Across all official senior matches, Lionel Messi has scored 936 goals and provided 408+ assists in 1,181 appearances (1.14 goal contributions per match), while Cristiano Ronaldo holds the all-time goalscoring record with 983 goals and provided 261 assists in 1,342 appearances (0.93 contributions per match). While Ronaldo leads the all-time goal tally (981 vs 936), Messi reached 936 goals in 161 fewer matches with a higher scoring ratio (0.79 vs 0.73), leads in non-penalty goals (826 vs 799) and assists (408+ vs 261), won the FIFA World Cup (2022) with two Golden Balls, and leads in Ballons d'Or (8 vs 5) and team trophies (47 vs 37).

Frequently Asked Questions: Messi vs Ronaldo

Who has more total career goals: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo holds the all-time world record with 981 official senior career goals in 1,342 appearances (0.73 goals per match), while Lionel Messi has scored 936 goals in 1,181 appearances (0.79 goals per match). Ronaldo leads by 47 goals, though Messi has played 161 fewer matches and maintains a higher scoring ratio.

Who has more non-penalty goals: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi has scored 826 non-penalty goals in 1,181 matches (0.70 non-penalty goals per game). Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 799 non-penalty goals in 1,342 matches (0.59 per game). Messi leads Ronaldo by 26 non-penalty goals despite playing 161 fewer career games.

Who has scored more free kicks: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi leads Cristiano Ronaldo with 76 direct free-kick goals to Ronaldo's 64. Messi has scored 50 for Barcelona, 12 for Argentina, 12 for Inter Miami, and 2 for PSG. Ronaldo has scored 33 for Real Madrid, 14 for Manchester United, 11 for Portugal, 5 for Al-Nassr, and 1 for Juventus.

Who has more career assists: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi leads Cristiano Ronaldo by 147 assists. Messi has recorded over 407 official senior assists (349 in club football and 58 for Argentina), making him the all-time assist leader in modern football history. Ronaldo has recorded 261 career assists.

Who has scored more penalties and what are their conversion rates? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 184 penalties from 221 attempts (an 83.3% conversion rate). Lionel Messi has scored 110 penalties from 141 attempts (a 78.0% conversion rate). Ronaldo has scored 74 more penalties than Messi.

Who has more career hat-tricks: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 66 career hat-tricks (56 in club football, 10 for Portugal). Lionel Messi has scored 62 career hat-tricks (48 for Barcelona, 11 for Argentina, 3 for Inter Miami). Ronaldo leads by 4 hat-tricks.

Who performed better in the FIFA World Cup: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, becoming the only player in history to win two FIFA World Cup Golden Balls (2014, 2022). In World Cup history, Messi has 21 goals and 12 assists in 34 games, including 7 goals in knockout rounds. Ronaldo has 11 goals and 2 assists across six World Cups, including his first knockout goal (against Croatia in 2026).

Who won more direct head-to-head matches between Messi and Ronaldo? ▾

In 36 official direct matches played between them, Messi's teams won 16, Ronaldo's teams won 11, and 9 ended in draws. Messi scored 22 goals in these matches; Ronaldo scored 21.

Who has won more trophies and Ballons d'Or? ▾

Lionel Messi holds the all-time world record with 47 senior team titles (including 10 La Ligas, 4 Champions Leagues, 2 Copas América, and 1 World Cup) compared to Ronaldo's 37 titles. Messi also holds the all-time record with 8 Ballons d'Or compared to Ronaldo's 5.

Why do most statistical analysts consider Messi the GOAT over Ronaldo? ▾

Statistical models favor Messi because of his dual dominance as both the world's elite goalscorer and its premier playmaker. Messi has a superior goal ratio (0.79 vs 0.73), more non-penalty goals (826 vs 799 in 161 fewer games), 147 more assists, superior minutes-per-goal involvement (72 min vs 88 min), and unparalleled international success crowned by the 2022 World Cup.
