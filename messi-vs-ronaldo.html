Frequently Asked Questions

Who has more official goals: Messi or Ronaldo?

Lionel Messi has 929 official senior goals compared to Cristiano Ronaldo's 924+ goals. Messi achieved this total playing 85 fewer career games.

Who has won more trophies: Messi or Ronaldo?

Lionel Messi is the most decorated player in football history with 46 official team trophies (including the 2022 World Cup, 4 Champions Leagues, 2 Copas América, and 10 La Liga titles). Cristiano Ronaldo has 35 team trophies.

Who won more head-to-head matches between Messi and Ronaldo?

In 36 direct matches played against each other, Messi's teams won 16, Ronaldo's teams won 11, and 9 were drawn. Messi scored 22 goals; Ronaldo scored 21.

How many Ballons d'Or do Messi and Ronaldo have?

Lionel Messi has won a record 8 Ballons d'Or (2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, 2023). Cristiano Ronaldo has won 5 Ballons d'Or (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017).
