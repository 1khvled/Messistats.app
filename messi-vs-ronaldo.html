Frequently Asked Questions: Messi vs Ronaldo

Who has more total career goals: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo holds the all-time world record with 979 official senior career goals in 1,337 appearances (0.73 goals per match), while Lionel Messi has scored 931 goals in 1,177 appearances (0.79 goals per match). Ronaldo leads by 48 goals, though Messi has played 160 fewer matches and maintains a higher scoring ratio.

Who has more non-penalty goals: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi has scored 822 non-penalty goals in 1,177 matches (0.70 non-penalty goals per game). Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 795 non-penalty goals in 1,337 matches (0.59 per game). Messi leads Ronaldo by 27 non-penalty goals despite playing 160 fewer career games.

Who has scored more free kicks: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi leads Cristiano Ronaldo with 68 direct free-kick goals to Ronaldo's 65. Messi has scored 50 for Barcelona, 11 for Argentina, 4 for Inter Miami, and 2 for PSG. Ronaldo has scored 33 for Real Madrid, 15 for Manchester United, 11 for Portugal, 5 for Al-Nassr, and 1 for Juventus.

Who has more career assists: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi leads Cristiano Ronaldo by 124 assists. Messi has recorded over 385 official senior assists (over 327 in club football and 58+ for Argentina), making him the all-time assist leader in modern football history. Ronaldo has recorded 261 career assists.

Who has scored more penalties and what are their conversion rates? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 184 penalties from 220 attempts (an 83.6% conversion rate). Lionel Messi has scored 109 penalties from 140 attempts (a 77.9% conversion rate). Ronaldo has scored 75 more penalties than Messi.

Who has more career hat-tricks: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 66 career hat-tricks (56 in club football, 10 for Portugal). Lionel Messi has scored 59 career hat-tricks (48 in club football, 10 for Argentina, 1 for Inter Miami). Ronaldo leads by 7 hat-tricks.

Who performed better in the FIFA World Cup: Messi or Ronaldo? ▾

Lionel Messi won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, becoming the only player in history to win two FIFA World Cup Golden Balls (2014, 2022). In World Cup history, Messi has 13 goals and 8 assists in 26 games, including 5 goals in knockout rounds. Ronaldo has 8 goals and 2 assists in 22 matches, with 0 goals and 0 assists in World Cup knockout stages.

Who won more direct head-to-head matches between Messi and Ronaldo? ▾

In 36 official direct matches played between them, Messi's teams won 16, Ronaldo's teams won 11, and 9 ended in draws. Messi scored 22 goals in these matches; Ronaldo scored 21.

Who has won more trophies and Ballons d'Or? ▾

Lionel Messi holds the all-time world record with 46 senior team titles (including 10 La Ligas, 4 Champions Leagues, 2 Copas América, and 1 World Cup) compared to Ronaldo's 35 titles. Messi also holds the all-time record with 8 Ballons d'Or compared to Ronaldo's 5.

Why do most statistical analysts consider Messi the GOAT over Ronaldo? ▾

Statistical models favor Messi because of his dual dominance as both the world's elite goalscorer and its premier playmaker. Messi has a superior goal ratio (0.79 vs 0.73), more non-penalty goals (822 vs 795 in 160 fewer games), 124 more assists, superior minutes-per-goal involvement (72 min vs 88 min), and unparalleled international success crowned by the 2022 World Cup.
