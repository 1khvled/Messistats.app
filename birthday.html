Frequently Asked Questions

Has Messi ever scored on his own birthday?

No. Messi was born on 24 June 1987. Late June corresponds to the European club off-season or rest intervals at international tournaments, meaning Messi has never played a competitive match on 24 June.

What is Messi's single most prolific calendar day?

Messi has scored 10 goals on both 2 May and 19 September. His 2 May tally includes his historic two-goal display at the Santiago Bernabéu in the 6-2 El Clásico of 2009.

How many days of the year has Messi scored a goal?

Messi has scored on 292 of 366 possible days (79.8% of the year), including leap day (29 February 2012 vs Switzerland, where he scored a hat-trick for Argentina).

Which month does Messi score the most goals in?

March is Messi's peak scoring month with 115 goals, followed by September (99 goals) and January (96 goals). July is the lowest (22 goals) due to summer club breaks.

Did Messi score on Christmas or New Year's Day?

No. Competitive leagues in Spain, France, and North America take winter breaks over Christmas and New Year's Day, so Messi has never played on 25 December or 1 January.

How is this goal dataset verified?

Every single one of the 927 goals is dated and cross-checked against primary match logs from FC Barcelona, PSG, Inter Miami, and the Argentine Football Association.
