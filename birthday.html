Frequently Asked Questions: Messi Birthday Goals

Has Messi ever scored on his own birthday?

No. Born 24 June 1987 in Rosario. Late June coincides with the European club summer break or international rest intervals.

What is Messi's single most prolific calendar day?

10 goals on both 2 May (including the 6-2 El Clásico) and 19 September.

How many days of the year has Messi scored a goal?

292 of 366 days (79.8% of the entire year), including leap day 29 February (hat-trick vs Switzerland in 2012).

Which month does Messi score the most goals in?

March is his peak month with 115 goals, followed by September (102) and January (96).

Did Messi score on Christmas or New Year's Day?

No. Official European and MLS fixtures pause for winter breaks on 25 December and 1 January.

How is this goal dataset verified?

All 936 goals are cross-referenced with primary match sheets from Barcelona, PSG, Inter Miami, and the Argentine FA.
